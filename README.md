# Mini-mechanical-water-dispenser-

## About the project

I made a small mechanical water dispenser using a small water pump, battery, switch and a water container.

The main idea was to make a simple dispenser which can move water when the switch is turned on.

When I turn on the switch, the pump starts working and moves the water. When I turn off the switch, the pump stops.

I made this project to learn about simple wiring, pumps and how electrical power can be used for a small mechanical project.

----

## Project Type

- Beginner hardware project
- Mechanical + electronics
- Manual water dispenser

----

## Main Features

- Small and simple design
- Battery powered
- Uses a small water pump
- Manual ON/OFF switch
- Easy to operate
- No Arduino or programming used

## How It Works

The battery gives power to the pump.

The switch is used to control the power.

When the switch is ON, the pump starts and moves the water.

When the switch is OFF, the pump stops.

----

### Simple connection

Battery → Switch → Pump

## Making Process

### 1. Planning

First I decided to make a small water dispenser. I made a simple idea of how the parts should be placed.

### 2. Collecting Parts

I collected the pump, battery, switch, wires and water container.

### 3. Assembly

I placed the pump with the water container and connected the required parts.

### 4. Wiring

I connected the battery, switch and pump with wires.

### 5. Testing

I checked the wiring and tested the pump with water.

### 6. Final Build

After testing, I fixed the parts properly and made the final version.

----

## Problems I Faced

While making the project, I had some small problems with the wiring and positioning of the pump.

I checked the connections and changed the position of the parts until it worked properly.
